The Process

Script to screen,
one continuous pipeline.

Five steps, always in this order — nothing skipped, nothing outsourced.

01

Script & Story

Drama, short films, episodic web series — built from a real narrative arc, not a loose idea.

02

Character & Location Design

A locked reference identity for every character before a single frame is generated — face, build, wardrobe, all fixed.

03

Shot-by-Shot Direction

Every scene broken into a shotlist — shot type, camera angle, movement — before generation starts.

04

AI Production

Generated in Google Flow, one shot at a time, checked against the character reference for drift.

05

Edit & Deliver

Cut, paced, and finished into a publish-ready piece — episode, ad, or standalone short.
